# Miroku Calendar: User Manual

**Web address:** https://miroku-calendar.vercel.app

**For:** members, editors and admins of Miroku Association Canada, Miroku Association USA and their centres.

The same guide is inside the app: sign in and click **Help** at the top of the page, or open
https://miroku-calendar.vercel.app/help.

---

## 1. Who does what

| You are… | You can… | Do you need an account? |
|---|---|---|
| **Member** | Read the calendar, print it, add it to your phone | No. You only need the link someone shares with you. |
| **Editor** | Everything a member can do, plus add, change and delete events, copy a month, publish a month | Yes: a Google account, approved by an admin |
| **Admin** | Everything an editor can do, plus manage groups, activities, calendars, people, and import from Excel | Yes: a Google account, approved by an admin |

Each organization (for example *Miroku Association Canada* or *Miroku Association USA*) has its own admins,
editors, groups, activities and calendars. A person can belong to more than one organization.

---

## 2. For members: reading the calendar

### Open the calendar
Use the link your centre or organization shares with you (by email, WhatsApp, etc.). It looks like
`https://miroku-calendar.vercel.app/miroku-canada/canada/2026/12`. You do not need to sign in.

- Use **‹** and **›**, or the month and year lists, to see other months.
- A month that is not ready yet shows *"This month is a draft"*.

### Add the calendar to your phone or computer
Click **Add to my calendar** (📅) and choose your device. You subscribe **once**: every month that is
published later appears on its own, and changes are updated automatically.

- **iPhone, iPad or Mac:** tap the option and then **Subscribe**. Activities appear in the Apple Calendar
  app, with a reminder one hour before each one.
- **Android or Google Calendar:** sign in with your Google account and press **Add**. The calendar then
  appears in the Google Calendar app on your phone. Google does not copy the reminder: to get one, open
  Google Calendar, go to *Settings → (this calendar) → Event notifications* and add one.
  - Google can take several hours to show new changes. This is normal.
- **Outlook:** sign in to Outlook and confirm. Outlook checks for changes a few times a day.
- **Another app:** choose **Copy the link for another app** and paste the link where that app asks for
  a calendar address ("subscribe by URL" or "add calendar from internet").

Times are shown in your phone's own time zone. For example, a Toronto activity at 7 PM Eastern shows at
4 PM on a phone set to Vancouver time.

### Print or save as PDF
Click **Print / Save as PDF**. The month fits on one landscape Letter page.
- To print: choose your printer and print.
- To get a PDF: in the print window choose **Save as PDF** (or *Microsoft Print to PDF* on Windows) as the
  printer.
- If the page looks too small or cut off, check that the print window says **Landscape** and the scale is
  **Default** or **100%**.

## 3. Getting access (editors and admins)

### Sign in
1. Go to https://miroku-calendar.vercel.app and click **Sign in to your organization**.
2. Choose your organization (for example *Miroku Association Canada*).
3. Click **Continue with Google** and choose your Google account.
   - There is no password to remember: your Google account is your sign-in.
   - The first time, this also creates your Miroku Calendar account.

### Ask to join
If you are not yet an editor of that organization, the app shows **Ask to join**.
1. Write a short note for the admin, for example *"I coordinate the Toronto group and will enter our
   activities."*
2. Click **Send request**.
3. Wait for an admin to approve you. When you sign in again after that, you go straight to the
   organization's **Events** page.

While you wait, the page shows **Waiting for approval**. You can **Withdraw** the request if you sent it
to the wrong organization.

> **Tip:** If you have several Google accounts (for example a personal Gmail and a centre account such as
> boston@miroku.us), use the one your admin expects. Each Google account is a separate person in the app.

### Sign out
Click **Sign out** at the top right.

## 4. For editors: the monthly work

After you sign in, open the **Events** tab of your organization. The left side is the form to add an
event; the right side is the month you are working on.

### Choose the month
On the right, use **‹ ›** or the month and year lists. Everything you see on the right is for that month.

### Start a new month quickly: copy from another month
Most months repeat the same pattern. In the box **Start from another month** (or **Copy events from another
month**):
1. Choose the month to copy from (the month before is chosen for you).
2. Click **Copy into (this month)**.

The pattern is kept: an activity on the 2nd Sunday goes to the 2nd Sunday, and weekly activities stay
weekly. Events already in the month are kept and nothing is doubled. Then add, change or delete what is
different this month.

### Add an event
1. **Date**: the day of the activity.
2. **Start** time and, if you want, **End** time.
3. **Group**: who holds the activity (the location and time zone come from the group).
4. **Activity**: the most recently used activity is already chosen.
5. *(Optional)* **More options**: a short **Note** in each language (shown after the activity name), or
   mark the event **Cancelled** (it is shown crossed out).
6. *(Optional)* **Repeat every week for the rest of this month**: adds the same event on the same weekday
   every week until the end of the month.
7. Check the grey **preview** box: it shows exactly how the line will look on the calendar, in each
   language.
8. Click **Add event**.

You only type the date, time, group and activity. The translations, the location and the colour are filled
in for you.

### Change or delete an event
In the list on the right, click **Edit** next to the event. The form on the left fills in; change it and
click **Save changes** (or **Cancel**). Click **Delete** to remove an event.

### Publish the month and share it
At the bottom right, **Calendars for this month** lists each calendar of your organization (for example the
national calendar and each centre calendar).

1. Click **View / print** to check the calendar as members will see it.
2. When it is ready, click **Publish**. The badge changes from *Draft* to *Published*.
3. Click **Copy member link** and paste the link in your email or message to members.

- Members only see published months. Before publishing, the link shows *"This month is a draft"*.
- Changes you make after publishing appear right away for members; you do not need to publish again.
- **Unpublish** hides the month from members again (for example if it was published too early).
- Phones that subscribed with **Add to my calendar** get only published months.

## 5. For admins: setting up your organization

Admins see four more tabs: **Groups & activities**, **Calendars**, **People** and **Import CSV**.

### 5.1 Groups & activities

#### Groups
A group is a place where activities happen (a centre, a group that meets in a public hall, a member's
home).

Click **Add a group** (or **Edit**) and fill in:
- **Short name**: what editors see in the Group list, for example *Toronto Centre*. It is also the name used
  by the Excel import.
- **Group name** and **Location**, in each language your calendars use (for example English and French).
- **Time zone**: choose from the list (Newfoundland, Atlantic, Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific, Alaska,
  Hawaii Time).
  - Leave it as **Same as the calendar (not shown)** when the group is in the calendar's own time zone.
  - Choose a time zone when the group is somewhere else, for example *Pacific Time* for Vancouver on the
    Canada calendar. The calendar then shows *"6 – 8 PM (Pacific Time)"*, translated into each language,
    and phones place the activity at the right hour.
  - **Other (type it below)** lets you type the words yourself, in each language, if your zone is not in
    the list.
- **Venue type**: *Centre*, *Public venue* or *Member home*. Events at a public venue get the "public venue"
  colour on the calendar.

**Remove** takes a group off the list. Past months keep showing it.

#### Activities
Click **Add an activity** (or **Edit**):
- **Short name**: what editors see in the Activity list.
- **Activity name** in each language. You type it once and it is reused for every event.
- **Add the group name**: adds the group to the line, for example *"Johrei Gathering – Calgary Group"*.
- **National service (blue)**: marks services for the whole organization. They get the blue colour, and
  centre calendars can show them too.

### 5.2 Calendars (languages, logo, banner)
One organization can have several calendars: one for the whole organization and one for each centre.
Each calendar has its own languages, logo, banner and open hours.

Click **Edit** next to a calendar, or **Add a calendar**:
- **Name**: for example *Toronto Centre*.
- **Web name**: the part of the link, for example `toronto` gives
  `/miroku-canada/toronto`. Use only lower-case letters, numbers and dashes, and avoid changing it after you
  have shared the link.
- **Languages**: **Main language (bold line)** and, if you want, a **Second language (grey line)**.
  Any combination of English, French, Portuguese and Spanish works: English only, French only, English +
  French, English + Portuguese, Portuguese + English, and so on.
  - When you add a language, remember to fill in that language for each group and activity on the
    **Groups & activities** tab.
- **Time zone of this calendar**: the zone of most of its activities (for example Eastern for Toronto).
- **Groups shown**: **All groups** for the national calendar. For a centre calendar, untick it and tick
  only that centre's groups. **Also show national services** adds the national activities.
- **Banner**: the title lines under the logo, in each language (the month title is added for you), the
  banner style, colours and the **Logo address**.
  - **Logo**: `/izunome-logo.png` is the Izunome logo, already in the app. For another logo, paste the web
    address of a picture (PNG or JPG) that is online, for example from your organization's website. Leave
    it empty to show no logo.
- **Open hours box**: the centre's opening hours in each language. Leave it empty to hide the box.
- **Colours and legend**: the colour and legend text for *National service*, *Public venue* and *Everything
  else*.

Click **Save calendar**, then open a month with **View / print** to see the result.

### 5.3 People: approving editors and admins
- **Asking to join**: people who signed in and asked to join appear at the top, with their note. Click
  **Approve as editor**, **As admin**, or **Decline**.
- **Give access**: you can also add someone ahead of time by typing their Google email and choosing
  **Editor** or **Admin**. They show as *"Invited, not signed in yet"* until they sign in with that Google
  account.
- **Remove** takes away someone's access. Their events stay.
- Members who only read the calendar do not need to be added: share the link with them.

Give the **Admin** role only to people who should manage groups, calendars and people. Keep at least two
admins per organization so the work never stops when one person is away.

### 5.4 Import CSV (from the old Excel calendar)
1. In Excel, open the **Entries** tab and choose **File → Save As → CSV UTF-8**.
2. The file needs the columns **Date, Start, End, Group** and **Activity**. Group and Activity must match the
   short names on the **Groups & activities** tab.
3. On the **Import CSV** tab, choose the file. The app shows how many events are ready and lists any rows
   that need fixing.
4. Click **Import (number) events**. Import each file only once, or the events are added twice.

### 5.5 New organization
An admin can create another organization (for example a new country) from the home page: click
**Miroku Calendar** at the top left, then **New organization**. Give it a name, a web name and its languages. You become its admin; next, add its groups
and activities, then set up its calendar.

## 6. Common questions

**I signed in but I only see "Ask to join".**
An admin of your organization has not approved you yet, or you signed in with a different Google account
from the one they approved.

**A member says the link shows "This month is a draft".**
The month is not published yet. An editor opens **Events**, goes to that month and clicks **Publish** next
to the calendar.

**I added an event but I don't see it in the list.**
The list shows only the month you are viewing. When you save an event in another month, the page moves to
that month so you can see it.

**The time is wrong on someone's phone.**
Check the group's **Time zone** on **Groups & activities** and the calendar's **Time zone** on
**Calendars**. Google Calendar can take several hours to pick up the fix.

**A translation is missing on the calendar.**
Fill in that language for the group or activity on **Groups & activities**.

**Can I undo a deleted event?**
No. Add it again. When in doubt, mark the event **Cancelled** (under *More options*) instead of deleting it.

**Who do I ask for help?**
An admin of your organization (the person who approved you). Admins can see everyone on the **People** tab.
